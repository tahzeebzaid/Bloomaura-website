How to Use – Skincare Routine

1

Cleanser

Start with a gentle cleanser to remove dirt, oil & impurities.

2

Toner

Apply toner to balance skin’s pH and tighten pores.

3

Serum

Use serum for targeted treatment like hydration or brightening.

4

Moisturizer

Lock in hydration with a lightweight or rich moisturizer.

5

Sunscreen

Finish with SPF to protect your skin from sun damage.

6

Eye Cream

Gently apply eye cream to reduce puffiness and dark circles.

7

Face Oil

Use a few drops of facial oil to nourish and lock in extra moisture.

8

Night Mask

Finish your routine with an overnight mask for deep skin repair.

9

Face Mist

Spritz a refreshing face mist throughout the day to keep your skin hydrated and glowing.

10

Lip Care

Finish your skincare by applying a nourishing lip balm to keep lips soft and smooth.
