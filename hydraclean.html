How to Use

  • ① Apply small amount to damp face
  • ② Massage gently in circular motion
  • ③ Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water
  • ④ Pat dry using a soft towel, avoiding harsh rubbing to protect skin barrier.
  • ⑤ Follow up with your favorite toner for best results.
  • ⑥ Finish with a moisturizer or sunscreen to lock in hydration.
